There's a seat for you

What really matters to you? Most people have questions they don't often say out loud.

About life. About meaning. About faith.

Alpha in Wilmslow is a space to ask questions, explore faith and share thoughts with others who are figuring things out too.

No pressure. No expectations. Just conversation. The teams of Life Church and Wilmslow Parish look forward to welcoming you.

Join Alpha Find Out More

What is Alpha?

Alpha is a series of relaxed evenings where people come together to eat, listen and talk.

Some people come with strong beliefs.

Others aren't sure what they think.

Many are just curious.

Wherever you're starting from, you are welcome.

A Typical Evening

What to expect

Eat

Each evening starts with dessert. It's a chance to arrive, relax and get to know people over something sweet.

Listen

There's a short talk exploring big questions around life and faith. Simple, honest and designed to start conversation.

Talk

This is the most important part. You can say as much or as little as you like. No right answers and no pressure to agree.

Who is Alpha for?

Alpha is for everyone. We're born curious — you should ask, explore, question.

  • ✓ Unsure what you believe
  • ✓ Coming back to questions you've not thought about in years
  • ✓ Sceptical but open to conversation
  • ✓ Simply curious

You don't need any background in church.

You don't need to know anything about the Bible.

You can just turn up.
